Impact of social protection policies on inclusive growth in Sub-Saharan Africa: evidence from a bias-corrected dynamic panel

Onyina, K., & Baye, R. S. (2024). Cogent Economics & Finance, 12(1), 2409421.

This study examines social protection policies as a driver of inclusive growth. Using a panel of 48 sub-Saharan African countries over 1990–2022 and the least-squares dummy variable corrected (LSDVC) estimator, we find that social protection policies significantly contribute to inclusive growth, consistent with a Rawlsian theory of justice.

Cash Transfer and the Labor Supply of Vulnerable Households and Individuals: Does Household Indebtedness Matter? A Triple DiD Analysis

Onyina, K., & Turkson, D. (2024). SSRN Working Paper.

Using a triple difference-in-differences model, we examine how household debt moderates the impact of Ghana's LEAP 1000 program on labor supply. LEAP 1000 decreases indebted beneficiaries' agricultural labor supply while increasing labor supply for non-farm enterprises, pointing to a role for financial management and debt relief in program design.
