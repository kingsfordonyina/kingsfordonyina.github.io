Impact of social protection policies on inclusive growth in Sub-Saharan Africa: evidence from a bias-corrected dynamic panel

Onyina, K., & Baye, R. S. (2024). Cogent Economics & Finance, 12(1), 2409421.

Using a panel of 48 sub-Saharan African countries over 1990–2022 and the LSDVC estimator, we find that social protection policies significantly contribute to inclusive growth, consistent with a Rawlsian theory of justice.

JournalGoogle Scholar

Cash Transfer and the Labor Supply of Vulnerable Households and Individuals: Does Household Indebtedness Matter? A Triple DiD Analysis

Onyina, K., & Turkson, D. (2024). SSRN Working Paper.

Using a triple difference-in-differences model, we examine how household debt moderates the impact of Ghana's LEAP 1000 program on labor supply — decreasing indebted beneficiaries' agricultural labor supply while increasing labor supply for non-farm enterprises.
