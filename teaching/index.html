Teaching

Teaching interests, courses, and my teaching philosophy.

I enjoy teaching applied econometrics and economics in ways that make quantitative methods intuitive and useful for real policy questions. My teaching interests span econometrics and causal inference, health and public policy economics, and principles of micro- and macroeconomics — with an emphasis on connecting theory to data and hands-on software practice.
