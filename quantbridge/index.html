About QuantBridge

QuantBridge grew from a shared commitment to making rigorous quantitative training and research opportunities more accessible to early-career scholars, particularly those with limited access to specialized methodological support. Its community now includes more than 310 researchers across over 20 countries.

To participate, collaborate, facilitate a session, or support the initiative, contact konyina@charlotte.edu or dturkson@unc.edu .
