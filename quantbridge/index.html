QuantBridge

QuantBridge is an applied quantitative webinar series I co-founded with Vanessa Owusu Ansah (Lancaster University) and Danny Turkson (UNC Chapel Hill). It supports early-career researchers working with quasi-experimental methods — with sessions especially relevant for scholars in economics, public policy, sociology, social work, and political science. Each topic is covered in two sessions: a theory session and a live software application session.

About QuantBridge

QuantBridge grew out of a shared commitment among its co-founders to making rigorous applied econometrics accessible to early-career scholars globally. The February 2026 edition attracted 150+ participants from over 17 countries, reflecting the breadth of interest in these methods across disciplines and regions.

For more information, to get involved, or to support this initiative, reach out at konyina@charlotte.edu or dturkson@unc.edu.
