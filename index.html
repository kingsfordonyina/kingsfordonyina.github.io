Welcome!

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy Analysis at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte and am on the 2026–2027 academic job market. My research studies how social policies shape the well-being of economically vulnerable households over the life course and across generations. My work spans the United States and sub-Saharan Africa.
