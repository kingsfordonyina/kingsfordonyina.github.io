Hello, welcome to my website!

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Public Policy Analysis at the University of North Carolina, Charlotte, and am currently on the 2026/2027 job market. My research lies at the intersection of social policy, international development policy, impact evaluation, and applied microeconometrics, with a focus on how public and private safety nets shape economic security, human development, and intergenerational mobility over the life course and across generations.
