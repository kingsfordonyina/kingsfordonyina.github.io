About Me

I'm a PhD candidate in Public Policy at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. I use applied microeconometrics to study social safety nets, international development, and how public policy shapes the well-being of economically vulnerable households — across both the United States and sub-Saharan Africa. My work aims to produce rigorous evidence that informs policies designed to improve opportunity and reduce disadvantage.
